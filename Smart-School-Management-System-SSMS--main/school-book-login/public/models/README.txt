Place your exported Tripo character model here as:

    character.glb

How to export from Tripo (studio.tripo3d.ai):
  1. Open your generated model.
  2. Click "Export" (bottom toolbar).
  3. Choose format: GLB (glTF Binary).  [GLB embeds textures in one file]
  4. Save the downloaded file here and rename it to exactly:  character.glb

Tip: the model in the screenshot is ~1M faces. If load feels slow,
re-export with a lower face count / enable mesh decimation in Tripo,
or keep "HD" rather than "Ultra" quality. Aim for < 25 MB if possible.

Until character.glb exists here, the app shows the SVG cartoon fallback.
